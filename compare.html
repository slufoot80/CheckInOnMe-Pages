The privacy difference

When a viral safety app is owned overseas, your name, your contacts, and your location are the price of admission. SafePulse has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. What leaves your device is only what an alert needs — your name, your contacts' numbers, and your last known location — used once to deliver that alert, never sold, and deleted when you delete your data.
