The Objective

Create a highly emotional, thumb-stopping vertical video that makes the viewer immediately think:

  • “I need this on my parent’s phone.”
  • “I need this for my spouse.”
  • “I should install this for myself.”

The emotion is concern and love — not graphic fear. We lead with a relatable worry (not hearing from someone you love), then resolve it with hope: the app helps people stay connected and get help sooner.

⚠  Important — responsible messaging (please follow exactly):
  • Do NOT claim or imply the app prevents harm, guarantees safety, or saves lives.
  • Do NOT show gore, blood, hospitals, ambulances, or medical/emergency-response scenes.
  • Do NOT exploit panic. Keep it emotional but tasteful — concern, not terror.
  • SafePulse does not replace emergency services — it simply lets someone know when you don’t check in. This line must appear in the video (Scene 6).
This keeps the ad compliant with TikTok, Meta, and Google advertising policies.

Style Direction

  • Cinematic, natural lighting
  • Fast cuts during the opening 3 seconds
  • Emotional piano with subtle suspense
  • Clean, large, highly readable captions
  • Realistic actors, everyday settings
  • No gore, no hospitals, no ambulances
  • Warm resolution in the final third
  • Sound-off friendly (works with captions only)

Target Audience

Adults 30–65 People with elderly parents Parents of college students People who live alone Spouses of lone workers Families with disabled relatives

Emotional Goal

The viewer should finish the video thinking:
“I hope I never need this… but if I do, I’ll wish I had it.”

Emphasize preparedness, care, and peace of mind — never a promise that the app prevents harm.

Deliverables & Handover

  • Primary: 1× final video, MP4, 1080×1920 (9:16), 30–45s, captions burned in.
  • Bonus (if offered): a 3–5s standalone hook clip for A/B testing thumbnails/openers.
  • Optional variants: a version with no on-screen text (for platforms that add their own), and a square 1:1 crop.
  • We will provide: the SafePulse logo, app screenshots/screen recordings, brand color (green #1a5d1a), and the tagline.
  • Please use royalty-free / licensed music and stock cleared for paid social advertising.
